## Is Shared Hosting Enough for You?

For most blogs, portfolios and small business sites, yes. Shared hosting means your site shares one server's resources with other accounts. YouStable uses CloudLinux account isolation to keep accounts separate, and the plans follow a [Fair Usage Policy](https://www.youstable.com/fair-usage-policy).

Think about a [VPS](https://www.youstable.com/vps-hosting/) when:

- Your site slows down during traffic spikes.
- You keep reaching your plan's CPU, memory or process limits.
- You need root access to install your own software.
- Your database has outgrown the 1000 MB limit.

When you are ready, YouStable's team moves your site to a VPS at no cost, with zero downtime. The Halloween sale covers VPS plans too.
